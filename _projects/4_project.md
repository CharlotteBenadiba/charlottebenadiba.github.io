**2024–2026 · Research at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev**

## Research question

Does incorporating age at diagnosis help identify genetic
associations that reproduce across different populations
and datasets?

## My contribution

Working with Nadav Rappoport, I investigated Cox and logistic
regression approaches using UK Biobank and All of Us data.

My work included data harmonisation, cross-cohort replication
analyses, statistical simulations and 1,000 matched resamples
to investigate differences between cohorts.

## Main finding

The analyses did not establish a universal advantage for Cox
regression. Simulations identified a specific benefit when
genetic effects influenced disease onset timing without changing
binary disease prevalence.

## Methods

R · Survival analysis · Logistic regression · Statistical simulation
· Reproducible research

## Research output

Manuscript: “When Can Age-at-Onset Information Contribute to
Reproducible GWAS Findings? A Cross-Biobank Evaluation of
Cox and Logistic Regression.”

Charlotte Benadiba and Nadav Rappoport.
