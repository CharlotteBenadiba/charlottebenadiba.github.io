**October 2026–present · Project in development**

## Motivation

People with limited movement may retain small voluntary finger
movements. Papotalk explores how these movements could provide
a way to compose written messages.

## How it works

The proposed interface uses surface EMG to detect muscle activity
associated with finger movements.

Combinations of movements are mapped to letters. For example,
activating the right and left thumbs selects the letter A.

## My contribution

I am developing the connection between EMG-based movement
detection and an alphabet-based communication interface.

The aim is to make the controls adaptable to the movements
available to each person.

## Current status

The project is in development. The movement-detection component
and the text-entry interface need to be evaluated individually
and as an integrated system.

## Design priorities

- Reliable recognition of intentional commands.
- Few accidental activations.
- Limited physical effort and fatigue.
- An understandable and customisable letter mapping.
