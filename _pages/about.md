I am a second-year Master’s student in **Software Engineering**,
with a background in computer science and mathematics.
I develop projects aimed at making communication and digital
interfaces more accessible to people with motor impairments.

My experience spans **computer vision for robotics**,
**software development** and **health data analysis**.
I have worked on integrating machine-learning models into robotic
systems and conducted research on the reproducibility of genetic
associations across biobanks.

I am currently working on two projects using **surface
electromyography (sEMG)**: **Silent Speech**, which explores
French silent-speech recognition from muscle activity,
and **Papotalk**, a communication interface based on small
finger movements.

Previously, I adapted a **Nintendo Nunchuk controller to operate
an iPad pointer**, exploring an alternative to touchscreen interaction.

I am open to research collaborations and professional opportunities
in assistive technology, accessible communication and rehabilitation.

If you are working on a related challenge, I would be happy to connect.
